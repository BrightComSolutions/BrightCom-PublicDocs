### Posted Sales Credit Memo – Matrix Format for Retail Variants (#27282)

Adds a new credit memo report that renders item variants in a matrix layout for retail scenarios. The matrix format is more compact and easier to read when a single item has many variants (sizes, colors, etc.) on the same credit memo, matching how variants are already presented in other retail documents.
